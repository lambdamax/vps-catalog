Notes & sources

Levels: 1 is each provider's cheapest plan. Levels 2 to 5 show the cheapest plan with at least 2, 4, 8 or 16 GB of RAM. When a provider's smallest plan already exceeds a level, the same plan appears again.

Excluded: Fly.io and Railway (metered billing, no fixed specs). DigitalOcean Premium NVMe Droplets start at $7 (AMD) and $8 (Intel) for 1 GiB, but full specs were not confirmed, so they are not listed. DigitalOcean figures are Basic Regular (SSD) Droplets.

Hetzner prices are the USD new-order prices from its June 15, 2026 price adjustment, for EU locations, excluding VAT and IPv4. On Oct 27, 2026, Hetzner's site showed the CX and CAX plans as currently unavailable to order, so check stock first. Hostinger prices need a 24-month prepay. OVH prices assume 12 months paid upfront; confirm on the OVHcloud VPS page before ordering. Vultr blocks automated checks of its pricing page, so Vultr figures for levels 2 to 5 come from third-party price trackers.

Sources: Hetzner price adjustment and cloud pages, Akamai (Linode) CPU pricing, DigitalOcean Droplet pricing, Hostinger VPS, OVHcloud US VPS, plus third-party trackers for Vultr.
